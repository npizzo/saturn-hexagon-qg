Momentum drag or thickness relaxation?

Four completed 1.5-layer QG runs compare momentum drag with layer-thickness relaxation, each with resting deep flow or a prescribed deep jet matching the initial upper jet component. All runs share the initial upper winds, central cyclone, small sixfold seed, 3,200-km deformation radius and planetary rotation; convection is off. Each spans 144.68 Earth days. This coarse preview shows six actual saved states selected from 26 per run, with shared field scales and diagnostics over the full integration. No pattern-speed fit is included.

Interpretation

  • The drag and relaxation rates give the same 100-Earth-day free-mode amplitude decay at the chosen radial-plus-sixfold scale: k*² = (1/jet width)² + (6/jet radius)². Their damping differs at other scales. This is an exploratory comparison, not a measured Saturn cooling time.
  • Momentum drag damps upper-layer velocity toward rest in the model frame. Thickness relaxation targets uniform layer thickness, ψeq = ψd. With a prescribed deep jet, that target can supply upper-layer mechanical energy; thermal damping and target work must be distinguished.
  • The deep jet has fraction 0 or 1 of the initial upper jet component. The prescribed deep cyclone is zero in all four cases. The lower flow is fixed, not an evolving second layer.
  • The target is axisymmetric and does not impose a hexagon. These seeded integrations test finite-time persistence; they do not demonstrate spontaneous sixfold selection, asymptotic stability or numerical convergence.
  • Six selected fields cannot resolve propagation between them. The 2,000-second diagnostics remain available privately for a later phase analysis; no drift fit or observational System III registration is claimed here.
  • These are exploratory QG analogues of the 2017 shallow-water setup. The inherited strong central cyclone can exceed the controlled small-Rossby-number QG regime. Divergent shallow-water dynamics are absent.
  • Automatic synchronization remains off. This is a manual publication of completed simulations; no new simulation was run for the gallery.

Field scales use the 24 selected saved states (six per run); the mode plot scale uses all saved diagnostic times.
